The problem

Workout apps tend to make you choose.

Speed, detail or useful insight. MuscleMetrics is being built so those do not have to be separate experiences.

Quick logging is useful, but not when the history you build gives you little context for your next decision.

02

Detailed, but awkward

03

Numbers, but no connection

Disconnected metrics are hard to act on when they are not tied back to the plan and the workout that produced them.
