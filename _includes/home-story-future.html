Our story

Built from a real frustration with workout tracking.

“I created MuscleMetrics because I wanted a training app that I would genuinely keep using. Too many workout trackers were either fast but shallow, or detailed enough to be useful but awkward during an actual gym session.

I wanted one place where I could build a proper training plan, record exactly what I completed and look back far enough to understand whether I was really improving.

MuscleMetrics started as a way to solve that problem for my own training. It has since grown into a larger goal: building a workout product that helps people stay consistent, understand their progress and make better decisions without unnecessary complexity.

The next stage is to connect that same training experience with personal trainers—giving coaches better tools without taking control away from how they choose to coach.”

Coach attention system

MuscleMetrics will help coaches identify clients who may need attention by bringing together useful signals such as missed sessions, incomplete workouts, check-in changes and stalled progress.

Signals should explain why a client was surfaced and suggest possible next actions. They do not diagnose a client or make coaching decisions automatically.
