The problem

Workout apps tend to make you choose.

Many apps are quick to use but too shallow to provide meaningful insight, detailed but frustrating during a real workout, or filled with disconnected numbers that do not help users make their next training decision.

01

Fast, but shallow

Quick logging is useful, but not when the history you build gives you little context for your next decision.

02

Detailed, but awkward

Deep tracking loses value if entering a set during a real workout feels like admin.

03

Numbers, but no connection

Disconnected metrics are hard to act on when they are not tied back to the plan and workout that produced them.

Our story

Built from a real frustration with workout tracking.

“I created MuscleMetric because I wanted a training app that I would genuinely keep using. Too many workout trackers were either fast but shallow, or detailed enough to be useful but awkward during an actual gym session.

I wanted one place where I could build a proper training plan, record exactly what I completed and look back far enough to understand whether I was really improving.

MuscleMetric started as a way to solve that problem for my own training. It has since grown into a larger goal: building a workout product that helps people stay consistent, understand their progress and make better decisions without unnecessary complexity.

The next stage is to connect that same training experience with personal trainers—giving coaches better tools without taking control away from how they choose to coach.”

FAQ

Clear answers, without overclaiming.

If something is planned rather than released, it is labelled that way.

Is MuscleMetric free?+

MuscleMetric’s core workout planning, logging, history and progress features are being made available without a paid-tier restriction.

Is MuscleMetric available on iPhone and Android?+

MuscleMetric is currently available for iPhone. An Android release is not presented here as available until a verified store release exists.

Does MuscleMetric create workout plans automatically?+

Users can create and manage structured plans. MuscleMetric does not claim AI-generated workout plans as a released feature.

Does MuscleMetric predict my performance?+

MuscleMetric focuses on recorded training behaviour and understandable progress trends rather than presenting uncertain readiness predictions as fact.

Is MuscleMetric Coach available?+

No. MuscleMetric Coach is part of the planned product roadmap and is not currently presented as a released feature.

Is my workout data private?+

Accounts are separated and access controls protect individual user data. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Can I submit feature suggestions?+

An in-app feedback system is planned so users can submit suggestions and report issues. Until a live feedback route is published, the website does not invent a contact endpoint.

Product principle

Sources & Methodology

MuscleMetric is designed so progress metrics can be explained in plain language rather than presented as opaque scores.

01
Use recorded training behaviour

Progress views should be grounded in completed workouts, exercises and set-level history rather than claims the app cannot observe.

02
Separate examples from user data

Charts on this website use illustrative sample values and are labelled as examples. They are not user statistics or product claims.

03
Prefer explainable trends

Volume, frequency, goal progress and historical comparison should remain understandable enough that a user can see what changed and why.

04
Avoid certainty where certainty is not justified

MuscleMetric does not present an unreliable readiness prediction as a fact about what a user will perform today.

This website section is intentionally descriptive rather than medical or coaching advice.
